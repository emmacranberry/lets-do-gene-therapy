let's do gene therapy

Not medical advice.

how do we work?

  • Proteins are machines made from a single string of building blocks
  • DNA stores the blueprint using the letters ATCG
  • Cells read that blueprint to build proteins

proteins are complicated

  • The good news: nature already made millions — just pick one
  • There is tons of research and many resources for finding a target protein

ok I have one

  • Now you need a delivery vehicle
  • Human DNA is linear, but DNA can also form a circle — a plasmid
  • A plasmid can carry multiple parts: target protein sequence, a “production” sequence, and more

plasmid design complete

  • Once designed, you can order the plasmid for a couple hundred dollars

package arrived

  • It shows up in a dry-ice dewar
  • Use well-documented practices to infect bacteria
  • Grow the colonies that survived and took up the plasmid

goo grown (media)

  • Kill the bacteria and extract your DNA
  • Test it
  • Use PCR to make more copies of the DNA

DNA media complete

  • From here you need a retrovirus to get the DNA into human cells
  • That is where it gets more difficult

real world example?

  • Making proteins like insulin
  • Proteins that synthesize compounds or medicines
  • Cancer therapies
